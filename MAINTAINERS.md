## Safety-critical responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for preserving the SC-001..SC-040 qualification evidence, reviewing unsafe-code boundaries, keeping formal-verification gates green, and ensuring releases are generated only by the gated release workflow.

A maintainer must not waive a failed safety qualification check merely to publish a release. Any exception requires a documented issue explaining the affected finding, impact analysis, compensating controls, and an explicit decision not to claim safety-critical qualification for that release.
